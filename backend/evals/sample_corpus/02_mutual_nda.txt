MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

1. SCOPE AND DEFINITION
The parties desire to evaluate a potential business partnership. "Confidential Information" encompasses any technical designs, product roadmaps, or financial metrics disclosed and labeled as proprietary.

2. DUTY OF CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party agrees to hold all disclosed materials in confidence for a period of two (2) years from disclosure date, applying reasonable care.

3. EXCLUSIONS
Confidentiality does not extend to information that is publicly accessible, independently developed, or obtained from a third party without breach.

4. ARBITRATION AND GOVERNING LAW
Any controversy arising from this NDA shall be submitted to confidential binding arbitration under the laws of the State of Delaware.
