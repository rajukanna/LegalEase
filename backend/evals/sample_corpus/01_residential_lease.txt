RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

1. PARTIES AND PREMISES
This Agreement is between Metro Living LLC ("Landlord") and Johnathan Doe ("Tenant"). Landlord leases to Tenant the premises at 100 Main Street, Apartment 4B.

2. TERM AND RENT
The lease term is twelve (12) months starting August 1, 2026. Monthly rent is $2,100.00, payable on the first day of each month. A late fee of $125.00 applies if rent is received after the 3rd day of the month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Tenant shall deposit $2,100.00 as security. Landlord may withhold funds for cleaning, unpaid utility balances, and repairs at Landlord's discretion.

4. ENTRY BY LANDLORD
Landlord reserves the right to enter the premises without notice during reasonable hours for inspection, repairs, or pest treatment.

5. EARLY TERMINATION
Tenant may not terminate early without paying a liquidated damages penalty of three (3) months' rent and forfeiting the entire security deposit.
